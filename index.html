02 · research
MS thesis · FAST-NUCES Islamabad · supervised by Dr. Muhammad Asif Naeem

MIRRL: a lightweight controller for reducing modality interference in multimodal LLMs

Multimodal LLMs can over-rely on one input when image and text disagree. MIRRL is a lightweight reinforcement learning controller that dynamically balances visual and textual signals to reduce this cross-modal interference. It runs within a 6GB GPU budget and adds about 0.4% trainable parameters.
